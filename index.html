Projects

Sales performance analysis

The question: Which products, cities and customers bring in the most revenue, and when do sales rise and fall?

Tools: Excel (VLOOKUP and pivot tables), PostgreSQL (joins, GROUP BY, SUM and AVG), Power BI (dashboard).

How I did it

I joined three tables (customers, products and transactions) into one, worked out revenue after discounts as quantity × unit price × (1 − discount), and checked the totals in Excel and SQL until they matched. Then I built a dashboard in Power BI with four summary numbers and four charts.

What I found

  • 43,363,200total revenue from 149 sales
  • 58.8%of revenue comes from Electronics
  • 48%of sales come from Kano, Abuja and Lagos
  • 43.8%of revenue comes from just five customers

Sales were strong in January, March and May (about 9,000,000 each) and weak in February, April and June (about 5,300,000 each). The number of sales stayed near 25 a month, so the swings came from the size of the orders. Accessories were 40% of the items sold but only 7.4% of the money.

What I would recommend

Keep the top Electronics products in stock, sell accessories together with big items, run offers in the slow months, and look after the top customers while finding new ones.

Data notes: one transaction with a product that does not exist in the product list was left out, and one order recorded as -2 items was treated as 2 items because it looked like a data entry mistake.
