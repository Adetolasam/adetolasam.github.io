About Me

I am a Data Analyst in Lagos, Nigeria. My journey began at DecodeLabs, where I learned to turn messy, real-world data into something I could trust: cleaning it, fixing duplicates and inconsistent formats, and checking it with Excel and Google Sheets. That work taught me that good analysis starts long before the charts.

Today, I work as a Data Analyst at Techbridge, where I take business data from raw records to clear answers. I merge and clean datasets, query them with SQL (PostgreSQL), and present what I find in Power BI dashboards and plain-language reports. I care most about the story behind the numbers, so decision makers can see what is happening and what to do next.

  • Excel and Google Sheets
  • SQL (PostgreSQL)
  • Power BI
  • Data cleaning
  • Dashboards and reporting
