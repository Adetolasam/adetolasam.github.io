About Me

I am a results-driven Data Analyst with approximately 1 year of experience working with data, supporting business operations, managing databases, and transforming raw information into meaningful insights that support better decision-making.

I am passionate about using data to solve business problems, improve operational efficiency, and help organizations make informed, data-driven decisions.

My technical skills include Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, and SQL, which I use for data cleaning, data entry and validation, data analysis, database management, reporting, and dashboard development. I enjoy working with datasets to identify trends, uncover patterns, monitor performance, and communicate findings in a clear and practical way.

Throughout my professional journey, I have gained experience across data analytics, data entry, database management, and technical education. At Baselink Services Limited and DecodeLabs, I worked as a Data Analytics Intern, developing my understanding of Data Analysis and Business reporting.

Beyond my technical abilities, I value attention to detail, problem-solving, continuous learning, and effective communication. I believe that a good Data Analysis goes beyond producing reports; it involves understanding business questions, ensuring data quality, identifying useful insights, and presenting information in a way that helps people take action.

My portfolio showcases selected projects that demonstrate my skills in Excel, Power BI, and SQL, including data cleaning, analytical reporting, interactive dashboards, and database queries.

I am open to opportunities to collaborate with businesses, work on data-driven projects, and contribute to teams that value analytical thinking and continuous improvement.

My goal is to turn data into clear insights, practical solutions, and measurable business value.
