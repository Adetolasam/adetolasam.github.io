About Me

I am an aspiring Data Analyst in Lagos, Nigeria. My journey began at DecodeLabs, where I built a strong foundation in data preparation, converting raw, inconsistent datasets into accurate, analysis-ready data by removing duplicates, standardizing formats and validating results in Excel and Google Sheets. This foundation shaped my approach: dependable analysis begins with data that has been properly cleaned and verified.

I worked as a Data Analyst at Techbridge, where I took business data from raw records to clear answers. I merged and cleaned datasets, queried them with SQL (PostgreSQL), and presented what I found in Power BI dashboards and plain-language reports. I care most about the story behind the numbers, so decision makers can see what is happening and what to do next.

My technical skills span Excel, Google Sheets, SQL and Power BI, and I use each one at a different stage of an analysis. In Excel and Google Sheets, I clean, standardize and merge raw data, remove duplicates and inconsistencies, and build summaries that turn thousands of records into clear figures. In SQL, I combine data across related tables and write structured queries that measure performance, turning raw records into reliable evidence for business decisions. In Power BI, I present the results in dashboards with clear key figures and charts that show trends and comparisons at a glance. Across all four tools, I follow one consistent process: prepare and verify the data, analyze it, present it clearly, and finish with recommendations that support decisions. I check every total across tools, record the data I change or leave out, and write my findings in plain language so that both technical and non-technical stakeholders can apply them with confidence.

  • Excel and Google Sheets
  • SQL (PostgreSQL)
  • Power BI
  • Data cleaning
  • Dashboards and reporting
